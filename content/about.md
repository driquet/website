Hello, I'm **Damien Riquet**, Lead Research Engineer at **Hornetsecurity** (formerly Vade), a Proofpoint company. I turn NLP research into email protection that runs at scale: from the first experiment to the detector that blocks phishing in production. I supervise PhD students with public research labs, publish our work at venues such as ACL, EMNLP and LREC, and since 2026 I lead the **AI Special Interest Group** at **M3AAWG**.

Before that: a PhD in network security at the University of Lille, then two years as a kernel developer at Stormshield. My go-to languages are Go and C.

Off work, I'm a proud father of two living near Lille, France. I play disc golf and video games, have been a geocaching reviewer since 2015, and never turn down a good pun.

Opinions on this site are my own, not my employer's.
