If you've been here before, you may notice things look a bit different. I spent some time reworking the site, with one goal: make it easier to find what you came for.

What changed:

- **A simpler homepage**: recent news and recent posts, as plain lists.
- **A [News](/news/) section**: announcements like papers, talks and conferences now live here, so [Posts](/posts/) only holds the long-form writing.
- **Tag filters** on both lists, to jump straight to a topic.
- **Series pages**: [Classifying Text Locally](/series/classifying-text-locally/) gathers my posts on classifying text without calling an LLM API, in reading order.

And the one I'm most excited about: **a newsletter**. You can now get new posts by email, a few times a year, no spam. [Subscribe here](https://buttondown.com/driquet), or use the form at the bottom of any post or news item. If you prefer feeds, [RSS](/posts/index.xml) still works.

Thanks for reading!
