In June, I flew to **Vancouver, Canada** for the **[M3AAWG](https://www.m3aawg.org/) 64th General Meeting**, held June 2 to 5, 2025.

This was my first M3AAWG on site. My first one was supposed to take place in Dublin, but COVID turned it into an online meeting. This time, I could finally meet people in person, and I enjoyed it so much that it is what motivated me to apply for the [AI SIG lead position](/news/m3aawg-ai-sig/).

I gave a talk:

> :uk: **Breaking and Securing LLMs: Understanding Information Leakage Risks and Defenses**

LLMs are trained on, and prompted with, data that is not meant to come back out. The talk looked at how sensitive information can leak from these models, and at the defenses that help keep it in.

Thanks to everyone who attended!

On a personal note, this was my first time on the west coast of Canada, and I was amazed by how much Vancouver packs in: a city, the ocean, forests and mountains, all within reach. I walked around **Stanley Park**, wandered through **Granville Island** and explored the city. I loved every bit of it.
